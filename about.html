About us

Compassion, backed by structure

The Balm is a nonprofit health and social care organisation helping vulnerable people and underserved communities across Nigeria live healthier, safer lives.

Our story

Why The Balm exists

A balm is something that soothes and heals. That’s what we want to be for people facing illness, poverty and isolation at the same time.

Our founder spent more than three decades as a Medical Social Worker in Nigerian hospitals. Again and again she saw that the hardest part of being sick and poor wasn’t only the treatment — it was the bills, the lost income, the fear, and being alone.

The Balm was set up to answer all of that together: medical help, emotional support and a real path back to independence. We work closely with hospitals, government agencies, other charities and community leaders so that help reaches the people who need it most.

Our vision

A healthy, fair society where vulnerable people and underserved communities can get good healthcare, social protection and real opportunities to live with dignity.

Our mission

To improve health and wellbeing through healthcare support, advocacy, community programs, strong partnerships and lasting social support for those who need it most.

Our values

Four letters we live by

Our name spells out what guides every decision we make.

B

Benevolence

Care and compassion for every person we serve, without conditions.

A

Accountability

Honest use of every naira, and open reporting on what it achieved.

L

Life & Dignity

Everyone deserves healthcare, support and respect — no exceptions.

M

Mutual Collaboration

We go further together with hospitals, government, charities and communities.

Our goals

What we’re working toward

1

Better access to healthcare

More people getting care and health information through outreach, prevention and direct medical support.

2

Support that lasts

Counselling, rehabilitation and welfare for women, children, young people, older people and people with disabilities.

3

Stronger partnerships

Working with hospitals, government and charities to improve health and social outcomes together.

4

Resilient communities

Training, empowerment and evidence-based programs that keep improving lives long after we leave.

Where we work

Rooted in Ota, growing across Nigeria

We’re based in the Progressive Egbusi community, off Oladele, Bells Drive, Ota, Ogun State. We focus first on areas with little access to healthcare or social support, in both towns and villages, and plan to expand to more states over time.

We serve women and girls, children and teenagers, young people, older people, people with disabilities, low-income families, and anyone facing health or emotional hardship.

Led by the community. Every program is shaped with local leaders and the people it’s for — through conversations, needs assessments and feedback — so it fits real needs and keeps working.

About the organization

Compassion, backed by structure

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention is a nonprofit and humanitarian organization improving health outcomes and quality of life for vulnerable individuals and underserved communities across Nigeria.

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention is a nonprofit and humanitarian organization committed to improving health outcomes and enhancing the quality of life of vulnerable individuals and underserved communities through sustainable health and social intervention programs. The organization was established in response to the growing need for accessible healthcare support, social welfare services, advocacy, and community-based interventions for people facing health, economic, and social challenges.

We focus on promoting holistic wellbeing by combining healthcare support with social development initiatives that empower individuals, families, and communities to live healthier, safer, and more productive lives. Through strategic partnerships, advocacy, capacity-building programs, and direct community interventions, we work to bridge gaps in healthcare access, social inclusion, rehabilitation support, and community reintegration.

As a health NGO in Nigeria, we work collaboratively with healthcare institutions, government agencies, civil society organizations, development partners, and community stakeholders to design and implement programs that respond to pressing societal needs.

Our Vision

To build a healthy, inclusive, and empowered society where vulnerable individuals and underserved communities have equitable access to quality healthcare, social protection, and sustainable opportunities for improved wellbeing and dignity.

Our Mission

To improve health outcomes and social wellbeing through innovative healthcare interventions, advocacy, community-based programs, strategic partnerships, and sustainable social support services that empower vulnerable populations and promote inclusive development.

Core values

Four principles that shape every program we run and every decision we make.

Major objectives

What we are working toward

1

Improve healthcare access

Expand access to quality healthcare services and health education for vulnerable and underserved communities through preventive healthcare initiatives, medical outreach programs, awareness campaigns and healthcare support interventions.

2

Sustainable social intervention

Design and implement programs addressing psychosocial support, rehabilitation, community reintegration, social welfare and the wellbeing of women, children, youths, elderly persons and persons with disabilities.

3

Advocacy & partnership

Promote community engagement and strategic partnerships that influence positive health and social outcomes, working with healthcare institutions, government agencies, civil society and development partners.

4

Community resilience

Strengthen sustainable development through capacity building, empowerment initiatives, evidence-based programming and inclusive interventions that improve quality of life and long-term social impact.

Geographic coverage

Where we work in Nigeria

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention operates within Nigeria, delivering health and social intervention programs to vulnerable and underserved communities across both urban and rural settings. We are based in the Progressive Egbusi community, off Oladele, Bells Drive, Ota, Ogun State, and use a community-based implementation approach that prioritises areas with limited access to healthcare services and social support systems.

Our target beneficiaries include women and girls, children and adolescents, youths, elderly persons, persons with disabilities, low-income households, and individuals facing health and psychosocial challenges. As part of our long-term vision, we aim to progressively expand across additional states in Nigeria.

Community-led by design. All interventions are implemented with strong community involvement — stakeholder consultations, needs assessments, inclusion of local leaders, and beneficiary feedback mechanisms — to ensure relevance, inclusiveness and sustainability.

About the Organization

Compassion, backed by structure.

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention is a nonprofit and humanitarian organization committed to improving health outcomes and enhancing the quality of life of vulnerable individuals and underserved communities through sustainable health and social intervention programs. The organization was established in response to the growing need for accessible healthcare support, social welfare services, advocacy, and community-based interventions for people facing health, economic, and social challenges.

We focus on promoting holistic wellbeing by combining healthcare support with social development initiatives that empower individuals, families, and communities to live healthier, safer, and more productive lives. Through strategic partnerships, advocacy, capacity-building programs, and direct community interventions, we seek to bridge gaps in healthcare access, social inclusion, rehabilitation support, and community reintegration.

We work collaboratively with healthcare institutions, government agencies, civil society organizations, development partners, and community stakeholders to design and implement impactful programs that respond to pressing societal needs. Our interventions are guided by compassion, equity, accountability, integrity, and a strong commitment to sustainable development.

Vision Statement

To build a healthy, inclusive, and empowered society where vulnerable individuals and underserved communities have equitable access to quality healthcare, social protection, and sustainable opportunities for improved wellbeing and dignity.

Mission Statement

To improve health outcomes and social wellbeing through innovative healthcare interventions, advocacy, community-based programs, strategic partnerships, and sustainable social support services that empower vulnerable populations and promote inclusive development.

Core Values

B.A.L.M

Four principles shape every program we run and every decision we make.

B

Benevolence

We are committed to showing compassion, care, and humanitarian support to vulnerable individuals and communities through responsive health and social interventions.

A

Accountability

We uphold transparency, integrity, and responsible management of resources while ensuring measurable and sustainable impact in all our programs and operations.

L

Life & Dignity

We value and protect human life by promoting equitable access to healthcare, psychosocial support, rehabilitation, and social protection services for all.

M

Mutual Collaboration

We believe in building strong partnerships with healthcare providers, government agencies, civil society organizations, development partners, and communities to maximize collective impact.

Major Objectives

What we are working toward

  1. Improve access to quality healthcare and health education for vulnerable and underserved individuals and communities through preventive healthcare initiatives, medical outreach programs, awareness campaigns, and healthcare support interventions.
  2. Design and implement sustainable social intervention programs that address psychosocial support, rehabilitation, community reintegration, social welfare, and the wellbeing of vulnerable populations including women, children, youths, elderly persons, and persons with disabilities.
  3. Promote advocacy, community engagement, and strategic partnerships that influence positive health and social outcomes through collaboration with healthcare institutions, government agencies, civil society organizations, development partners, and community stakeholders.
  4. Strengthen community resilience and sustainable development through capacity building, empowerment initiatives, evidence-based programming, and inclusive interventions that improve quality of life and promote long-term social impact.
Geographic Coverage

Where we work

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention operates within Nigeria, with a focus on delivering impactful health and social intervention programs to vulnerable and underserved communities across both urban and rural settings. We are based in the Progressive Egbusi community, off Oladele, Bells Drive, Ota, Ogun State, and adopt a community-based implementation approach that prioritizes areas with limited access to healthcare services, social support systems, and development opportunities.

Our target beneficiaries include women and girls, children and adolescents, youths, elderly persons, persons with disabilities, low-income households, and individuals facing health and psychosocial challenges. As part of our long-term vision, we aim to progressively expand our geographic footprint across additional states in Nigeria and, where possible, beyond.

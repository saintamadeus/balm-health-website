Programs & Thematic Areas

What we actually do

Community-centered, evidence-based programs designed to address the health, economic, and social challenges facing vulnerable populations and underserved communities.

Where we are today: TBHISI was incorporated in November 2025. The programs below describe our operating model and intervention areas as defined in our organizational strategy. As specific outreach events, partnerships, and beneficiary numbers are delivered, we will publish real dates, locations, and outcomes here rather than placeholder statistics — see our Financial Transparency page for the same honesty applied to our finances.

1

Public Health Promotion & Preventive Healthcare

We promote preventive healthcare practices and community health awareness through health education, sensitization campaigns, screenings, and outreach programs targeted at improving health-seeking behavior and reducing preventable disease.

  • Community health awareness campaigns
  • Preventive healthcare education
  • Health outreach and screening programs
  • Hygiene and sanitation promotion
  • Nutrition and wellness education
  • Non-communicable and communicable disease awareness
  • Maternal, newborn, and child health education
2

Healthcare Support & Medical Assistance

We provide support initiatives that improve access to healthcare services for vulnerable individuals and underserved communities, particularly those facing financial, social, or systemic barriers to healthcare access.

  • Medical assistance and referral support
  • Healthcare access interventions
  • Support for vulnerable patients and families
  • Community-based healthcare initiatives
  • Linkages to healthcare providers and institutions
  • Health emergency response support where applicable
3

Mental Health & Psychosocial Support (MHPSS)

Recognizing the growing need for mental and emotional wellbeing services, we implement psychosocial support interventions aimed at promoting mental health awareness, emotional resilience, rehabilitation, and social reintegration.

  • Mental health awareness campaigns
  • Psychosocial support services
  • Counselling and emotional support initiatives
  • Trauma-informed community interventions
  • Rehabilitation and reintegration support
  • Community-based support networks
4

Women, Children & Vulnerable Population Support

We prioritize the protection, empowerment, and wellbeing of women, children, youths, elderly persons, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups through inclusive social intervention programs.

  • Women and girl-child empowerment initiatives
  • Child welfare and protection programs
  • Support for persons with disabilities
  • Social protection and welfare support
  • Gender-responsive community interventions
  • Vulnerable household support services
5

Community Development & Social Intervention Programs

We design and implement social intervention initiatives that strengthen community resilience, improve livelihoods, and address social challenges affecting vulnerable populations.

  • Community empowerment initiatives
  • Social welfare interventions
  • Community reintegration programs
  • Capacity-building and life skills training
  • Poverty alleviation support initiatives
  • Humanitarian and relief interventions where necessary
How we implement

Community-based, partnership-driven

Direct engagement

Community-based outreach, stakeholder consultations, needs assessments, and inclusion of local leaders and community actors.

Strategic partnerships

Collaboration with healthcare institutions, government agencies, civil society organizations, and development partners to strengthen reach and effectiveness.

Monitoring & learning

Routine field monitoring, beneficiary data collection, baseline and end-line evaluations, and continuous program improvement.

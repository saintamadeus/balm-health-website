{{icon:star}} Founder & Executive Director

Comfort Oluremilekun Samuel

A Medical Social Worker and public servant with more than 35 years of experience supporting patients and families in Nigeria’s public hospitals.

She began her career in the Audit Department of the National Orthopaedic Hospital, Dala, Kano State, before moving to the National Orthopaedic Hospital, Igbobi, Lagos. There she rose through the Medical Social Services Department — providing counselling, patient advocacy and welfare support to vulnerable people.

She holds a Diploma in Public Administration from Bayero University, Kano, a B.Sc. in Social Work from the University of Ibadan, and a Master’s in Medical Social Work from Lagos State University. She leads The Balm’s strategy, programs and day-to-day operations.

Legal status

Registered and accountable

The Balm is registered in Nigeria as Incorporated Trustees under Part F of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020. This gives us legal recognition to run programs, raise funds, form partnerships and work in communities.

Our registration numbers, certificates and compliance status are all on the transparency page, with scans of the real certificates.

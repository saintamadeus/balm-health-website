Get help

You don’t have to face this alone

If you, or someone you know, is struggling with illness, hospital bills, or a hard time at home, reach out. We will listen. Our help is free.

Emergency? If someone is in danger or seriously ill right now, call 112 (Nigeria’s emergency number) or go straight to the nearest hospital. We are not an emergency service.

Reach us

Call us, or send a WhatsApp message

Call first. If nobody picks up, please don’t give up — send us a WhatsApp message and we will reply as soon as we can.

Who we help

We may be able to help if…

We are a small, new organisation, so we can’t help everyone — but every person who reaches out is listened to.

  • {{icon:cross}}You or a family member is sick and can’t afford treatment or a referral.
  • {{icon:bed}}Someone is in hospital with little or no support from family.
  • {{icon:brain}}You are struggling emotionally after illness, loss or a hard experience.
  • {{icon:basket}}Your household can’t cover food or basic needs this month.
  • {{icon:people}}You care for an older person or someone living with a disability and need support.
What happens next

Four simple steps

  1. 1

    You reach out

    By call, WhatsApp or a visit. Tell us a little about what’s going on.

  2. 2

    We listen

    Someone from our team talks with you to understand your situation.

  3. 3

    We meet you

    Where needed, we visit or meet you to understand the full picture.

  4. 4

    We tell you honestly

    What we can do, or who else can help if it’s something we can’t do ourselves.

{{icon:handshake}}

Asking for someone else?

Hospitals, churches, mosques, neighbours and family can tell us about someone who needs help. Please ask that person first if they’re happy for you to contact us.

{{icon:shield}}

Our help is free

We will never ask you to pay to receive help. If anyone claims to be from The Balm and asks you for money, don’t pay — tell us.

{{icon:lock}}

Your story stays private

What you tell us is kept confidential. We never share your story or photo without your written permission. See our safeguarding policy.

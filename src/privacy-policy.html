Privacy Policy

What information we collect through this website, why, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention (“TBHISI”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. We handle personal information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Things you tell us: your name, email, phone number and message when you contact us, sign up for updates, apply to volunteer, or tell us about a gift.
  • Gift details: the amount, date and reference code of a bank transfer you report to us. We never ask for, and never store, your card or bank login details.
  • Basic technical data: our web host may record standard information such as IP address and browser type to keep the site secure. We do not run advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To confirm and acknowledge gifts
  • To reply to messages, volunteer applications and partnership requests
  • To send updates, only if you asked for them
  • To meet our legal and financial reporting duties as a Nigerian nonprofit

Who processes it

Forms on this site are delivered to us through our website host, Netlify, which processes the data on our behalf. When online card payments are added, they will be handled by a licensed payment provider (such as Paystack) on its own secure systems — not ours. We do not sell your information, ever.

How long we keep it

We keep gift records for as long as Nigerian law requires for financial records. We keep other messages only as long as we need them, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Photos and stories

We never publish a photo or story of a child or vulnerable person without documented consent (or a guardian’s consent), as set out in our Safeguarding Policy.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop receiving emails. Use the details on our Contact page.

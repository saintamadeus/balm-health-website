Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people using screen readers, magnifiers or a keyboard only.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

What we’ve done

  • We aim for WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the international standard, including on our forms.
  • Every form field has a clear label and plain-language error messages.
  • Text and background colours are chosen to meet AA contrast levels.
  • Menus, buttons, tabs, accordions and forms all work with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Meaningful images have text descriptions; decorative ones are hidden from screen readers.
  • A “skip to main content” link sits at the top of every page.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, all animation is switched off.

Honest status

This is a careful, good-faith effort — not a claim of full compliance. We have not yet completed a full automated audit and a complete screen-reader test of every page. That is on our list, and we’ll update this page when it’s done.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, email thebalmhealthinitiative@gmail.com or call +234 907 852 4319. Tell us the page and what happened, and we’ll fix it.

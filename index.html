What we do

Five thematic areas, one mission

We combine healthcare support with social development initiatives so individuals, families, and communities can live healthier, safer, and more productive lives.

Public Health & Preventive Care

Health education, screenings, sanitation promotion, and disease-awareness campaigns.

Healthcare Support & Medical Assistance

Medical referrals, healthcare-access interventions, and support for vulnerable patients and families.

Mental Health & Psychosocial Support

Counselling, trauma-informed care, rehabilitation, and community reintegration support.

Women, Children & Vulnerable Populations

Protection and empowerment programs for women, children, youths, elderly persons, and persons with disabilities.

Community Development

Empowerment initiatives, life-skills training, and poverty-alleviation support rooted in local ownership.

Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning

Evidence-based programming with routine monitoring and periodic evaluation to keep us accountable.

Founder & Executive Director

Led by three decades of frontline healthcare service

Comfort Oluremilekun Samuel is a Medical Social Worker with over 35 years of experience across Nigeria's public healthcare system, including the National Orthopaedic Hospitals in Kano and Lagos. She holds a Diploma in Public Administration, a B.Sc. in Social Work from the University of Ibadan, and a Master's degree in Medical Social Work from Lagos State University.

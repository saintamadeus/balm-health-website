Why we exist

For a poor family, illness is never just a medical problem. It is the bill, the lost wages, the fear — and the loneliness of a hospital ward.

The Balm was set up to treat all of it, not just the part a prescription can fix.

Medical help

Treatment costs, referrals and health outreach for people who can’t afford care.

Emotional support

Counselling and companionship, because recovery is harder when you feel forgotten.

A way back

Skills, tools and support so people leave our care able to provide for themselves.

How OPTRTE works

Not a handout. A whole journey.

Most help stops at relief. OPTRTE stays with one person through five stages until they no longer need us.

People are chosen at random, not by application — so help reaches those who would never think to ask for it.

The full program

Pick

We find someone in urgent need — often a person no other program has reached — and take them in.

Treat

We pay for and arrange the medical care their condition needs, start to finish.

Rehabilitate

We support their physical and emotional recovery, so they come back stronger, not just discharged.

Teach

We train them in a skill or trade they can actually earn from.

Empower

We set them up with the tools or starter support to provide for themselves — and then we step back.

Who leads us

What we do

Five thematic areas, one mission

We combine healthcare support with social development so individuals, families and communities across Nigeria can live healthier, safer and more productive lives.

01

Public Health & Preventive Care

Community health education, sensitization campaigns, screenings and outreach programs that improve health-seeking behaviour and reduce preventable disease.

02

Healthcare Support & Medical Assistance

Medical assistance, referral support and healthcare-access interventions for vulnerable patients facing financial, social or systemic barriers to care.

03

Mental Health & Psychosocial Support

Counselling, trauma-informed community interventions, rehabilitation and reintegration support for those carrying invisible burdens.

04

Women, Children & Vulnerable Groups

Protection and empowerment programs for women, children, youths, elderly persons and persons with disabilities across underserved communities.

05

Community Development

Empowerment initiatives, life-skills training and poverty-alleviation support built on local ownership and participatory development.

06

Monitoring & Evaluation

Evidence-based programming with routine monitoring, baseline and end-line evaluation — so our impact is measured, not just claimed.

Founder & Executive Director

Three decades on Nigeria’s healthcare frontline

Comfort Oluremilekun Samuel is a Medical Social Worker with over 35 years of service across Nigeria’s public healthcare system, including the National Orthopaedic Hospitals in Kano and Lagos.

She holds a Diploma in Public Administration from Bayero University, a B.Sc. in Social Work from the University of Ibadan, and a Master’s degree in Medical Social Work from Lagos State University — a career built on patient advocacy, psychosocial support and welfare services for vulnerable individuals.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention ("TBHISI", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide directly: name, email address, phone number, and any message content when you contact us, apply to volunteer, or make a donation.
  • Donation information: when our payment gateway (Paystack) is live, payment card and transaction details will be processed directly by Paystack under its own security standards — we do not store your full card details on our servers.
  • Automatically collected information: once analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) are enabled on this site, we may collect standard technical data including IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, to understand how the site is used.

How we use your information

  • To process donations, acknowledge gifts, and issue receipts
  • To respond to enquiries, volunteer applications, and partnership requests
  • To send updates about our programs, if you have opted in to hear from us
  • To improve this website and understand how visitors use it
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including those under Nigerian nonprofit and financial-reporting law

How we protect your information

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Payment processing, once activated, will run through Paystack's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure rather than our own servers.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate this website, process payments, or manage donor communications, and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of TBHISI, our beneficiaries, or others.

Photos and stories from our programs

Where a photo or story involves a minor or a vulnerable individual, we obtain documented consent (or guardian consent) before publishing, in line with our Safeguarding Policy.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct, update, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you communications, by contacting us using the details on our Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our operations, tools, or legal obligations change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at thebalmhealthinitiative@gmail.com or +234 907 852 4319.

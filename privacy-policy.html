Privacy Policy

How The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention (“TBHISI”, “we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide directly: name, email address, phone number and message content when you contact us, apply to volunteer, or make a donation.
  • Donation information: when our payment gateway (Paystack) is live, payment card and transaction details will be processed directly by Paystack under its own security standards — we do not store full card details on our servers.
  • Automatically collected information: once analytics tools are enabled, we may collect standard technical data including IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited.

How we use your information

  • To process donations, acknowledge gifts and issue receipts
  • To respond to enquiries, volunteer applications and partnership requests
  • To send updates about our programs, if you have opted in
  • To improve this website and understand how visitors use it
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations under Nigerian nonprofit and financial-reporting law

How we protect your information

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. Payment processing, once activated, runs through Paystack’s PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure rather than our own servers.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate this website, process payments or manage donor communications, only to the extent necessary. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of TBHISI, our beneficiaries or others.

Photos and stories from our programs

Where a photo or story involves a minor or a vulnerable individual, we obtain documented consent (or guardian consent) before publishing, in line with our Safeguarding Policy.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct, update or delete personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you communications, using the details on our Contact page.

Contact us

For privacy-related questions, contact thebalmhealthinitiative@gmail.com or +234 907 852 4319.

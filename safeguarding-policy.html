Safeguarding & Child Protection Policy

Our commitment to protecting children and vulnerable individuals across every program we run in Nigeria.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention works directly with vulnerable individuals and underserved communities, including women, children, youths, elderly persons and persons with disabilities. Protecting the safety, dignity and wellbeing of everyone we serve — and everyone who represents us — is a non-negotiable condition of our work, not an optional add-on.

Our commitment

  • We have zero tolerance for abuse, exploitation or harassment of any beneficiary, staff member, volunteer or community member connected to our programs.
  • Every staff member, volunteer, consultant and intern is expected to uphold this policy as a condition of working with us.
  • Concerns raised in good faith will be taken seriously, investigated and acted on — and no one will be penalised for raising a genuine safeguarding concern.

Photo and story consent

If any photo, video or story used in our communications or on this website involves a minor or a vulnerable individual, we obtain documented consent — or guardian consent where the individual is a minor or lacks capacity to consent — before that content is published anywhere, including social media. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by contacting us.

Working with children and vulnerable groups

  • Program staff and volunteers working directly with children or vulnerable populations are expected to conduct themselves professionally and in line with this policy at all times.
  • Interventions involving minors or vulnerable individuals are designed with appropriate consent processes and, where relevant, guardian or community leader involvement.
  • We are building out formal background-check and training procedures as part of our institutional strengthening — see our transparency page for our broader honesty about where our systems are still maturing.

Reporting a concern

If you have a safeguarding concern about any TBHISI program, staff member or volunteer, contact us directly and confidentially at thebalmhealthinitiative@gmail.com or +234 907 852 4319, marking your message “Safeguarding”. All concerns are treated seriously and handled with discretion.

Governance

Safeguarding and protection standards are promoted at organizational level as part of our human resources and program management practice, with oversight from the Board of Trustees and the Executive Director.

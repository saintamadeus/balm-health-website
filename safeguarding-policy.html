Policy

Safeguarding & Child Protection

Keeping children and vulnerable people safe is a condition of everything we do — not an add-on.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

To report a concern: email thebalmhealthinitiative@gmail.com or call +234 907 852 4319 and mark it “Safeguarding”. Every concern is taken seriously and handled in confidence.

We work directly with women, children, young people, older people and people with disabilities. Protecting the safety, dignity and wellbeing of everyone we serve — and everyone who works with us — comes first.

Our commitment

  • Zero tolerance for abuse, exploitation or harassment of anyone connected to our work.
  • Every staff member, volunteer, consultant and intern must follow this policy to work with us.
  • Concerns raised in good faith are investigated and acted on, and no one is punished for raising one.

Photos and stories

No photo, video or story showing a child or vulnerable person is published anywhere — including social media — without documented consent, or a parent or guardian’s consent where needed. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by contacting us.

Working with children and vulnerable people

  • Staff and volunteers must behave professionally and in line with this policy at all times.
  • Programs involving children or vulnerable people include proper consent and, where relevant, involve guardians or community leaders.
  • We are putting formal background checks and training in place as we grow. See our transparency page for where our systems are still developing.

Who oversees this

Safeguarding is overseen by the Executive Director and the Board of Trustees as part of how we manage people and programs.

This is where we’ll share what happens on every outreach — who we met, what changed, and what it cost. We’re just getting started, so here’s the story so far.

Our journey so far

From an idea to a registered organisation

Every step below is real and checkable. We’ll keep adding to it.

See our registration papers
  1. 17 November 2025

    Registered with the CAC

    The Balm is incorporated as Incorporated Trustees under Part F of CAMA 2020, with a seven-member Board of Trustees.

  2. 18 December 2025

    Registered with SCUML

    We complete registration with the Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering under the EFCC, and obtain our Tax Identification Number.

  3. 2026

    Website goes live

    We publish our programs, leadership, policies and certificates so anyone can check who we are before they give.

  4. Coming next

    Our first Reach the Sick visit

    Our first hospital visit is being planned. When it happens, the story will appear right here.

Our promise

How we will tell people’s stories

The people we serve are not props. These rules apply to every photo and word we publish.

Consent first

No photo or story goes up without written permission — from a parent or guardian for children. Anyone can ask us to take theirs down.

Dignity, not pity

We show people as they are — strong, capable, going through a hard time. We change names when someone prefers privacy.

Real numbers

Every update says how many people were reached and what it cost. If something didn’t work, we’ll say that too.

Be the first to know

Get the first story in your inbox

We’ll email you when our first outreach happens — and a few times a year after that. Nothing more.

Leadership & Board of Trustees

Who leads this Nigerian health NGO, how decisions are made, and how the organization is structured — laid out plainly, not buried in a footer.

Founder & Executive Director

Comfort Oluremilekun Samuel

Founder & Executive Director

Comfort Oluremilekun Samuel is a seasoned public service professional, humanitarian, and experienced Medical Social Worker with over three decades of dedicated service in healthcare administration, patient support services, and social welfare interventions in Nigeria.

She began her career at the National Orthopaedic Hospital, Dala, Kano State, in the Audit Department, later transferring to the National Orthopaedic Hospital, Igbobi, Lagos State, where she progressed within the Medical Social Services Department — providing psychosocial support, patient advocacy, counselling and welfare services to vulnerable individuals.

She holds a Diploma in Public Administration from Bayero University, Kano State, a B.Sc. in Social Work from the University of Ibadan, and a Master’s degree in Medical Social Work from Lagos State University. With over 35 years of experience, she provides strategic leadership and oversight for the organization’s programs and operations.

Board of Trustees

Highest governing body

The Board of Trustees provides strategic leadership and institutional direction, approves organizational policies, ensures accountability and regulatory compliance, oversees financial management and risk control, supports resource mobilization, and promotes organizational sustainability and ethical standards.

As recorded on our incorporation documents, the duly appointed Trustees of The Balm Health Initiative and Social Interventions (TBHISI) are:

  • Isaac Samuel
  • Olubunmi Ajegunmo
  • Tunde Olokode
  • Israel Samuel
  • Comfort Samuel — Founder & Executive Director
  • Alaba Simpson
  • Oluwole Omoyele

Trustee names are transcribed directly from our incorporation documents. Individual professional affiliations will be published as the Board formalises its public bios.

Legal status

Registered and accountable

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention is registered with the appropriate government regulatory authorities in Nigeria as Incorporated Trustees under Part F of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020, giving it legal recognition to operate, implement programs, mobilize resources, establish partnerships and execute community-based interventions.

Full registration numbers, certificates and compliance status are published on our Regulatory Compliance & Financial Transparency page, alongside scans of the actual certificates.

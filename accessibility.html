Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including users of assistive technology.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology such as screen readers, screen magnifiers or keyboard-only navigation.

What we have done

  • Targeting WCAG 2.1 Level AA as our conformance standard, including for donation and contact forms.
  • Every form field is paired with a visible, properly associated label, with inline error messaging.
  • Semantic HTML with a logical heading hierarchy on every page.
  • Every text/background colour pair in the design system has been programmatically audited against WCAG AA thresholds (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text). All 28 audited pairs pass.
  • Keyboard-navigable menus, buttons, accordions and forms with visible focus states.
  • Descriptive alt text on meaningful images; decorative images marked so screen readers skip them.
  • A “skip to main content” link at the top of every page.
  • Full support for the prefers-reduced-motion setting — all animation is disabled for users who request it.

Honest status

This is a good-faith, documented effort — not a claim of full conformance. Colour contrast has been formally audited and passes AA. However, we have not yet run a full automated accessibility scan (such as an axe-core audit) or a complete manual screen-reader pass across every page. That testing is on our post-launch checklist and this statement will be updated once complete.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, tell us at thebalmhealthinitiative@gmail.com or +234 907 852 4319, including the page and what happened. We will work to fix it.

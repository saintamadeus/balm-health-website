Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 12 July 2026

The Balm Health Initiative and Social Intervention is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, or keyboard-only navigation.

What we've done

  • Targeting WCAG 2.1 Level AA as our conformance standard, including for forms such as our donation and contact forms.
  • Every form field is paired with a visible, properly associated label.
  • Semantic HTML with a logical heading hierarchy on every page.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background across the site's green/red/ink palette.
  • Keyboard-navigable menus, buttons, and forms, with visible focus states.
  • Descriptive alt text on meaningful images; decorative images are marked so screen readers skip them.
  • A "skip to main content" link at the top of every page for keyboard and screen-reader users.

Honest status

This is a good-faith, documented effort — not a claim of full conformance. We have not yet run this site through an automated accessibility scanner (e.g. an axe-core-based tool) or a manual screen-reader pass end to end; that testing is part of our pre-launch checklist and this statement will be updated once it's complete.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, please tell us at thebalmhealthinitiative@gmail.com or +234 907 852 4319, including the page and what happened. We'll work to fix it.

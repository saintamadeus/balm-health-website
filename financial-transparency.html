Regulatory Compliance & Financial Transparency

This is the one page on this site where our registration numbers and certificates live. Trust is built on what is disclosed, not what is claimed — including what we do not have yet.

Honest starting point: The Balm Health Initiative and Social Interventions (TBHISI) was incorporated with the Corporate Affairs Commission on 17 November 2025. As a newly registered organization, we do not yet have an audited financial statement or a completed annual report to publish. We are stating that plainly rather than making claims we cannot back up. As soon as our first financial year closes and is reviewed, the report will be published on this page.

How we intend to allocate funds

We have not run a full financial year yet, so we will not publish a program-versus-admin split we cannot verify. What we commit to, and can be held to publicly, is this:

  • Donor and grant funds are tracked against the specific program or intervention they were given for, wherever a donor designates one.
  • Our Finance & Administration Unit and Board of Trustees provide oversight on financial management, budgeting, procurement and risk control.
  • Once our first annual accounts are prepared, we will publish income, expenditure and the program-to-administration ratio on this page — the actual numbers, not a promotional summary.

Financial sustainability approach

To reduce dependency on any single funding source, our strategy includes grant acquisition and donor engagement, strategic fundraising, corporate partnership development, community and philanthropic support mobilization, program sponsorship and collaborative financing, in-kind contributions, and long-term partnership agreements.

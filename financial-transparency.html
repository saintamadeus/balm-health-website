Transparency

See the papers. Follow the money.

Trust comes from what we show, not what we say. Here are our registrations, the real certificates, and an honest account of what we don’t have yet.

Honest starting point: The Balm was registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission on 17 November 2025. Because we are new, we do not yet have an audited financial statement or annual report. We’d rather say that plainly than make claims we can’t back up. Our first report will be published here as soon as our first financial year closes and is reviewed.

How we handle money

Our promises about your gift

We haven’t completed a full financial year, so we won’t publish a spending split we can’t prove. Here is what we commit to — and you can hold us to it:

  • If you choose a program when you give, your gift is tracked against that program.
  • Every gift made through our donate page is matched to a bank record and personally acknowledged.
  • Our Finance & Administration unit and Board of Trustees oversee budgets, purchases and risk.
  • When our first accounts are ready, we’ll publish income, spending, and how much went to programs versus running costs — the real numbers, not a glossy summary.

How we’ll stay funded

So we never depend on one source, we’re building a mix of:

  • Grants and donor partnerships
  • Gifts from individuals
  • Company partnerships and sponsorship
  • Community and church support
  • Donated goods and services
